# Credits

## Textures and models

All textures and models in `web/assets/tex` and `web/assets/models` come from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under
[CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) (public domain, no attribution needed).
They are fetched and scaled for the web by `scripts/fetch-assets.py`, which
lists every asset by its Poly Haven id. Thanks to the Poly Haven artists and
supporters.

## Fonts

- [Monoton](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Monoton) by Vernon Adams, and
- [Rubik](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rubik) by Hubert and Fischer,

both under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org),
self-hosted in `web/assets/fonts` (Latin subsets from Google Fonts).

## Sound

Every sound is synthesized in the browser (`web/sfx.js`); there are no
sound files.
