# Credits

Everything below is free to use. `scripts/fetch-assets.py` fetches and
prepares it; the results are committed under `web/assets`.

## Models

- **KayKit Furniture Bits, Restaurant Bits and City Builder Bits** by
  [Kay Lousberg](https://kaylousberg.com), released under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`web/assets/kk`). Source: the KayKit-Game-Assets repositories on GitHub.
- **Ultimate Modular Men and Ultimate Modular Women** by
  [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), released under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`web/assets/people`, license text included).
- Everything else (bar stools, casino tables, slot machines, the back bar,
  the tap, lamps, signs, the skyline) is built in code (`crates/client/src`).

## Textures

- Floors and walls: generated in code (`crates/client/src/look.rs`).
- The night sky (`web/assets/sky/night.jpg`): "Qwantani Moonrise (Pure Sky)"
  from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

## Fonts

- [Monoton](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Monoton) by Vernon Adams, and
- [Rubik](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rubik) by Hubert and Fischer,

both under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org),
self-hosted in `web/assets/fonts` (Latin subsets from Google Fonts).

## Sound

- Footsteps, glass, chips, cards, coins, punches, the bell and the door are
  from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s Casino Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface
  Sounds and RPG Audio packs, released under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`web/assets/sfx`, converted to MP3).
- The ambience, the jukebox's songs and the stings are synthesized in the
  browser (`web/sfx.js`).
